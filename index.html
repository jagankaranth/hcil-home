Meet Mycem and Zuari, two cement brands from Heidelberg Materials India, each with a distinct range for the way people build.

Mycem Cement

Built around the family building their own home, once. Power Shield has finer particles that restrict water moving through the pores, so walls resist seepage and efflorescence. Primo comes in a tamper-proof pack that holds quality past the point Indian practice asks for a retest.

Building India with less carbon
in every bag of cement.

Cement is hard to decarbonise. We're not using that as an excuse. There are only three levers that genuinely move the carbon in a bag, and we pull all three.

What goes into the blend

Composite cement now carries 41% clinker where PPC carries 61%, and it is roughly a quarter of everything we sell. Less clinker is less limestone burnt, and burning limestone is where most of cement's carbon comes from.

What we burn

10.63% of our kiln fuel is now alternative fuel rather than coal, up from 8.1%. Waste heat from cooling is recovered and put back to work, 67,040 MWh of it last year.

Where the power comes from

43% of our electricity comes from solar, wind and recovered heat, including a 5.5 MW wind and solar hybrid commissioned in central India last year.

Beyond the plant boundary

Afforestation at Damoh, water recharge across our mining sites, and Five Star rated mines at Patharia. Net zero by 2050, on targets validated by the Science Based Targets initiative.
